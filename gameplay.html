Learn The Hunt:

Starting with queing; when hoping into a match of Bounty Hunt you are given the choice to play Solo, Duo, or Trio. When playing Solo and Duo you also have the choice of how big enemy teams are, either Duos or Trios. Players can only que aganist larger team sizes. (i.e Duo and Solo can que against Trio but Trio can't que into Duo lobbies)

Hunt's main gamemode: Bounty Hunt, is broken into three main phases: "Hunt-Banish-Extract".

First, upon spawning into one of the four playable maps, teams will be placed along the outer edge of the map. In the top left corner there will be the given target(s) for the round. There are three main sources of Bounty: Bosses, Wild Targets, and Sealed Bounty. (Refer to the Boss and Wild Target pages for more information)

(Sealed Bounty Tokens can spawn in single Boss target matches. These are bought with Pledge Marks [See the Loadout page for more information about Pledge Marks] at "event loactions" or in the absence of an event location, the Clockmaster's Supply.)

Once the match begins, Hunters will find Clues for information on a Boss target. These "Clues" are visable though Darksight and show a bright blue light leading to their location. As a team grabs Clues, the locations on the map will begin to gray out; narrowing the locations the Target can be. Each Boss Target requires three Clues to be revealed; alternatively, Boss Targets will be shown upon Banishment or coming within close enough range of their Compound (This includes grabbing the Clue within the Boss Target's Compound).

Once the Boss Target is located, it's time to put them down. After the Boss is killed, a team must begin Banishment to spawn the two Bounty Tokens. (Banishing takes over three minutes, except in the case of Wild Targets and Sealed Bounty Tokens which are one token that is available instantly) Upon beginning Banishment, every Hunter is alerted to where the Boss Lair is located. The Lair is visable on the map and through Darksight.

After the Bounty Tokens are dropped, any Hunter that pickes up a Bounty Token (Boss Target, Wild Target, Sealed Token, or off a Hunter's courpse) gains five seconds of Dark Sight Boost.
