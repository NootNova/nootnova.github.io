Hire Your Hunter:

Hunters are of two classes: Hired and Recruited.

The Hired Hunters cost 100 Hunt-Dollars and come with no equipment but have 10 Trait points.
Recruited Hunters are free, come with a basic Contraband loadout and only one Trait, no Trait points.
(Contraband is any item [Weapon, Tool, or Consumable] that has the red-skulls icon attactched to it; this is applied to Free Hunter loadouts and items acquired in a match and makes these items not able to be sold.)

Traits:

Traits are used to gain an tactical advantage and to specialize your Hunter.

There are currently 65 total Traits.

They are broken into three main types: Normal, Scarce, and Burn. Some Traits also have Conditional effects. The two current Conditions are being Solo and having the "Catalyst" Trait.

The Trait UI has different icons to quickly show information; diamonds on the bottom left of an Trait show if its affected by Solo play, (White) a Burn Trait, (Orange) or a Scarce Trait (Blue). The last UI element is in the top-right of a Trait, an Orange circle means it is affected by the Catalyst Trait. (Note that Traits can be under multiple classes)

Normal Traits are bought once and stay equiped until you remove them or the Hunter is Killed/Reitred. Burn Traits have a Use and after that Use they are gone and either need to be repurchased or found in match again. Scarce Traits are Traits with strong effects, they are only able to be aquired in a match or through specific interactions.
